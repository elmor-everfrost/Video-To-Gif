# Video-To-Gif
Just a simple tool to convert exported clips to gifs, cuz the free sites pss me off witht he watermarks

**Select Video** — chuck in your mp4/mov/webm/whatever. It'll load up and show you the player.
 
**Play/scrub area** — hit play or drag the seek bar to check out your clip. Click and drag right on the video to draw your crop box. It grabs pause automatically so it doesn't wiggle on you mid-drag. Type exact numbers in the X/Y/W/H boxes if you want exact borders, or just hit reset to go back to full frame.
 
**High-Res Export** — tick this if you want your crop exported at full quality, no shrinking. Looks great but the file gets chunky, so keep your crop tight if you flip this on.
 
**Resolution Scale** — how much to shrink your cropped area (ignored if High-Res is on). Lower % = smaller file, blurrier gif. 50% is usually a decent middle ground.
 
**FPS** — how many frames per second get captured. Higher = smoother motion but way bigger file. 10-15 is usually plenty for a gif, don't need 30 unless it's fast action.
 
**Quality** — 1 is best looking but slowest to render, 20 is fastest but kinda crunchy. Live somewhere in the 3-8 range for most stuff.
 
**Dithering** — smooths out color banding/gradients a bit, costs you a slightly bigger file. Worth turning on if your gif looks blotchy.
 
**Start Conversion / Stop & Render** — self explanatory, stop just cuts it short and renders whatever you've captured so far.
